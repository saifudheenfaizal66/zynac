01 The Plight

Why Traditional Aquaculture Loses Yield

Freshwater fish farms lose up to 30% of their potential yield to blind feeding schedules, sudden shifts in water quality, and daily guesswork. Too often, farmers only spot trouble after a crisis has already started, leading to wasted feed, lost stock, and unpredictable income.

02 The Solution

Introducing FinFloat

Zynaq Aqua pairs autonomous surface robotics with practical water-sensing intelligence to take the guesswork out of pond management. FinFloat stays out on the water, reading pond conditions and acting on its own to keep fish healthy and growth on track, while our core hardware design stays under wraps until launch.

Less guesswork on the bank. Steadier water in the pond. Built from the ground up in Kerala for real freshwater farms.

03 Purpose & Direction

Mission, Vision & Core Objectives

Our Vision

"To make sustainable, precision freshwater aquaculture practical and dependable for every fish farmer."

Our Mission

"To pair autonomous robotics with reliable water intelligence, replacing daily guesswork with steadier, healthier ponds."

OBJECTIVE 01

Eliminate Blind Feeding

Cut feed waste and protect water quality by delivering feed based on what is actually happening in the pond.

OBJECTIVE 02

Prevent Stock Loss Early

Move fish farming away from late-stage firefighting toward steady, continuous pond care that protects farmer livelihoods.

OBJECTIVE 03

Empower Smallholder Farmers

Build rugged, energy-autonomous hardware suited to real Indian freshwater conditions and priced for everyday farm adoption.
